#+title: l-land-1

You who are of my kithfolk, read with haste and write much the same!

A force of five thousand Thrakish muskets and some ten or so ships has forced entry to the Kar by the Pass of Heroes and took Zaroon and Fort Sands. Fort Collum fired upon the invaders, but has now been put to the gun and shall surely surrender before this letter even makes it to your hand. Beyond what is believed a quite accurate account of troop numbers, we have no knowledge of what occurs within the city after the occupation, though a trio of exploring officers have been sent to remedy this.

A small militia has already been raised from Kibroz, and a thousand persons that escaped Zaroon before its occupation have formed into a regiment of volunteers, though only around half are at this moment well-armed. These Zaroonites have taken to the task of securing the Red Line railroad bidge over the Kar. When added to the garrison of Forts Corg and Harfington This brings our forces in the vicinity of Zaroon to number just over two and one half thousand.

You may, of course, order the raising of more soldiers though I must impress upon you the fact that our most able and willing people are on the borders and in Myrkon.

I have, just as I write this, been informed that the Mayor of Brimisora has minutes ago opened the Moth's Cavern Arsenal, and you will consequently have a number of artillery pieces to distribute as you see fit amongst the fortifications and city defenses under your command. These guns are old, from the Third Musagari War at the latest, and large enough to be likely of no use to a moving column, but by the first count there are at least fifty that should be immediately serviceable and perhaps another two dozen more that could be restored to safe working order.

Amongst the greatest threats we face at the moment is the Thrakish seamen, as mentioned there is a squadron of several ships in the Kar, and a letter from the Customs Court collating reports from lighthouses insists on a surely hyperbolic total of forty-six ships and boats in the Kara'Bindara and Goobinschtyle area.

Our own naval presence is, for the moment, three Customs Court cutters in Goobinschtyle and a mere handful of gunboats that have been pirate-hunting from River City to the Green Sea. You do have full authority to purchase or hire out any number of the countless fishing and transport boats of our gracious rivers, though to crew them may prove difficult and to find a use for them beyond as fireships doubly so.

My dear and fair lordship, we have been taken by surprsise, but the greatest heroes of all Lacron have come from the muddy waters of the deltas. May the spirits of those heroes smile upon us now, and may we not disappoint. Azuago!
