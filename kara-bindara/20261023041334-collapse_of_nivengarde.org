#+title: Collapse of Nivengard
In 1711, after finally achieving a state that could be loosely called victory in the Twenty Year Rebellion that had been eroding his control of the eastern reaches of his domain and the concurrent 13th Kaedazan-Inasagi War, Lord Kaedaz was publicly executed by his royal guards at the behest of his consort [come up with name]. She took the throne after a nearly year-long period of contested succession. Her rule was largely characterized by a deterioration of the tax system leading to increased opulence of the upper classes as the military rested comfortably on its laurels. In 1732 Nivengard received a diplomatic visit from the newly crowned Lord Narascorides of Thrakal. As was the trend among Thrakalian adventurers of his generation, he challenged the queen to a duel as a compliment to her beauty. This duel ended with him fleeing the nation after beheading their ruler in front of a crowd. 

As a result of the instability brought by the chaotic successions in the continent’s largest superpower, several other nations on the continent managed to ally together and bring down the crumbling giant of Nivengard. After thoroughly balkanizing the former superpower to prevent its resurgence, the coalition quickly fell apart and former allies of convenience turned on each other. The period following the Collapse of Nivengard is often called the  [[id:8bbe1e0a-c89c-45b6-ba7c-4e6708bb9569][Violence without Victors]]
