#+title: Rose
Sprambis Rose is a Valestherne-educated scion of a rich Cisani family (textile mill owners) and former lawyer-turned-Assembly member for the Lesser Assembly-turned-freebooter and nationalist thought leader.
Shortly after losing election to the Thrakal Assembly, he decided to attempt to destroy the Pirate Republic of Zaquür to set up a pro-Thrakal state using a private army raised mostly with money from political campaign contributions. This was nor particularly successful, but the group found more victory from fighting as more normal mercenaries in service to local warlords & corporations like  [[id:5f4e54f8-2e55-445f-b5f8-dd48d579bdff][TAHC]].
