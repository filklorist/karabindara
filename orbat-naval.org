* Great Gulf Squadron
An oder but prestigious group elevated from the Gulf Mouth Force so important during the previous Musagari Wars. Has not had much modernization of late.
Mostly quick, "deep" water frigates joined by a pair of early steam corvettes for patrol and shallow water operations

* West Gulf Blockading Squadron
Task organized from ships in drydock at the war's start, meaning those not able to be used in the initial tasking & battles but available by the time the blockade was being organized.
Contains a just-launched screw-driven frigate and a somewhat ancient ship-of-the-line that was for a long time being repaired. Otherwise mostly older, smaller ships in for repair in refit.

* Coast Penatrative Squadron
A newly put together group for coastal & riverine service, drawn from other units to make a purely steam-capable squadron. The mortar schooners are all grain and ore shipping boats refitted during the first years of war.
Composed of a few ships, two of the three semi-experimental Koros-class iron-hulled gunboats, and a smattering of other gunboats and mortar vessels
