#+title: Timeline Leading Up to 1781

* ~1670
First Lacronian & Thrakish colonies on Musagar
* ~1680
First Musagari War
Grew out of border skirmishes between farmers, pioneers, woodsmen
Temporarily cemented Lacron as dominant force in Musagar
* 1708-1711
Second Musagari War
Much more conventional war
Nivengard sent navy to aid Thrakal in exchange for forces to help in Inasag (Jind Expedition)
Nivengard frigates siezed Lacron's biggest port, all Lacron forces surrendered
Citizens and soldiers were given chance to evacuate or assimilate, most left
Thrakal left as sole power on the island
Demonstrated importance of navy in fighting over an island, creating what would become the famously competent Musagari shipbuilding industry
* 1725-1726
Third Musagari War
